# Ten-minute outside-user comparison (not yet conducted)

**Purpose:** Determine whether the evidence-provenance workflow helps participants choose independently sourced verification channels in synthetic impersonation cases compared with a normal job-scam safety checklist.

**Ethics & safety:** Two voluntary adult participants only, no recruitment from actual victims, no passwords/private communications, all companies fictitious. No recording without consent, and no fabricated outcomes.

## Materials

- Condition C — Generic checklist: Do not pay up front, do not share login codes, verify employer contact through official channels you locate independently; be skeptical of urgency.
- Condition O — OfferProof synthetic one-click demo: use the **Run full evidence demo** on the app's Overview screen.
- Case A: The recruiter offers a July internship and includes a seemingly official job listing link. A note found from that link agrees with the message; another separately located contact note describes different role/timing.
- Case B: The recruiter offers the same position, but two "confirmation" notes each ultimately derive from the recruiter-provided source.

## Task

Show a participant the case without commentary. Ask exactly:

> What would you do before replying or sending any documents? Which source do you think independently corroborates the message?

Record their action and reasoning verbatim, not just whether they clicked the "right" answer.

## Counterbalanced assignment

- Participant 1: Case A with checklist C, Case B with OfferProof O.
- Participant 2: Case A with OfferProof O, Case B with checklist C.

Stop after at most five minutes per condition. Do not coach. Debrief both participants afterward.

## Scoring rubric

A case counts as correct only if the participant (a) refuses to treat recruiter-provided links as independent confirmation and (b) chooses an employer contact channel located separately to verify the sender. Do not mark as successful if they merely say the job listing exists, or if they assume the app itself verifies the sender.

## Reporting

If and only if the sessions are performed, write "n of 2 correct under OfferProof vs n of 2 under checklist" with exact observations, limitations, and order. This sample is too small for a general effectiveness claim. No valid statistical significance can be inferred. Report observed confusion and failures even if this weakens the pitch.

## What would falsify our thesis?

- Participants interpret a green/independent node as a guarantee the website is real.
- People do not understand the original-message dependencies without explanation.
- The checklist produces the same or better independent verification decisions.
- Groq-generated comparisons distract from correct independent-channel decisions.
